## How a project runs

Every project goes through the same four stages. You're part of each one, from the first conversation.

### 1. Discovery

We start by understanding your business, your audience and what success looks like. You leave this stage with goals, an audience and success criteria written down, and a shared understanding of the scope.

### 2. Planning

Next, the idea gets a structure: a practical roadmap with priorities, wireframes and a technical approach. This is where I tell you which parts are simple, which are risky, and what I'd build first.

### 3. Development

I build in working increments, and you see and use them as they land rather than waiting for one big reveal. Feedback throughout the build is how we catch a wrong assumption while it's still cheap to change.

### 4. Delivery

Testing, final refinements, launch, and ongoing support after it. Launch day isn't the end of the relationship.
